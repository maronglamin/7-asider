7a-side Field Partnership Proposal

7a-side helps football players discover and book available fields while giving field owners a simpler way to receive bookings and grow usage.

Commercial terms:
- For every booking hour completed through 7a-side, 7a-side earns GMD100.
- The field owner keeps the remaining booking revenue according to the field's listed price.

Merchant account requirement:
The field must maintain a merchant account with at least one supported local wallet provider: APS, Wave, or Yonna. This helps make customer payments and field payouts easier to manage.
